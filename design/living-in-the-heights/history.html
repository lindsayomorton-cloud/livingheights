History

Houston’s first planned suburb

Oscar Martin Carter founded the Heights as its own town in 1891. Here are the people who built it, the names who grew up here and the places that still define it.

The founders

Who built the Heights

Founder

Oscar Martin Carter

Founded the Heights in 1891

His Omaha and South Texas Land Co. bought 1,756 acres

Founder

William G. Love

First mayor of Houston Heights, 1896

Led the town from incorporation

Founder

William A. Wilson

Platted Woodland Heights in 1907

Built it as a streetcar suburb

Founder

William C. Hogg

Developed Norhill starting in 1920

Sold Proctor Plaza to the city for $1

Founder

Sunset Heights Realty Co.

Laid out Sunset Heights in 1910

Created Aug. 15, 1910
