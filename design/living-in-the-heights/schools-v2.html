Finding your school

How zoning works

A few steps before you buy, rent or enroll.

  • Start with the addressSchool zones follow the exact street address, not the neighborhood name.
  • Check the district’s school finderHouston ISD lists the zoned elementary, middle and high school for every address.
  • Ask about programsMagnet, Montessori and dual-language programs can mean a different school than the zoned one.
  • Visit in personTour the campus and ask how pickup works on your street.
