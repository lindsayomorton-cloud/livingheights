Motion

{{ c.label }}
  • Intro, 0–2s: the Draper Real Estate logo settles in from 90% on cream with “Houston, Texas” below, then fades out over 0.5s to reveal the scene.
  • Lauren’s box rises 80px and settles in 0.8s, ease-out.
  • Words appear one line at a time; oxblood type on cream, the spoken word sits on a bluebonnet highlight in cream and upcoming words wait at 40%.
  • The spotlight scales up from 60% with a small overshoot (0.6s), holds for about 2s, then glides into the center grid slot (0.6s). The side photos pop in 0.3s apart. All three drop out together.
  • Everything moves straight up, down or in scale. Nothing tilts or spins.
