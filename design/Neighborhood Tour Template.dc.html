Motion

{{ c.label }}
  • The hand-lettered neighborhood name fades up in the word zone over the establishing shot.
  • The trail draws in below the arch: five circles on a dashed bluebonnet line, popping in 0.15s apart.
  • The current stop fills bluebonnet and grows to 120%; stops already visited stay filled at full size.
  • Each stop holds about 3.4 seconds and crossfades over 0.5s with a slow push-in.
  • For the close, the trail drops away and Lauren’s round clip pops up in its place. Nothing tilts or spins.

Filming notes

  • Open on a street sign, an esplanade or a long view down a block.
  • Film each stop vertically with the subject in the middle 690px. Ask before filming inside a business.
  • Lauren’s outro is a 3–4 second clip, framed head and shoulders, ideally filmed in the same neighborhood.
