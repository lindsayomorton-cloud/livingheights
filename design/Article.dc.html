What to know before your first offer in the Heights

Deed restrictions, flood history and historic-district rules can shape a home’s value. Here is what to check before you write an offer.

Lauren DraperRealtor · Houston Heights

Heights homes carry a lot of history, and some of it shows up in the paperwork. A few checks before you write an offer can save you from surprises after closing.

Check the flood history

Look up the address on FEMA and Harris County flood maps, and ask the seller for their flood disclosure. White Oak Bayou runs along the edge of the Heights, so elevation varies from block to block.

Read the deed restrictions

Many Heights blocks have deed restrictions that limit lot use, setbacks or commercial activity. Your title company can pull them, and it is worth reading them before you commit.

Know if it’s in a historic district

Parts of the Heights sit in city historic districts. Exterior changes there need approval, which matters if you plan to add on or replace windows.

Confirm the schools

School zones follow street lines, not neighborhood names. Confirm the zoned HISD schools for the exact address.
