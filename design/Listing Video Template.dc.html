Motion

{{ c.label }}
  • Each room holds about 3 seconds and crossfades over 0.5s. The footage pushes in slowly (about 6%) while it holds.
  • The room name sits on a cream pill at the bottom of the arch and changes with each cut.
  • Beds, baths and square feet pop up from 60% with a small overshoot, 0.25s apart, and stay through the backyard shot.
  • For the close, the numbers drop away and Lauren’s round clip pops up in their place while the caption asks for a showing.
  • Everything moves straight up, down or in scale. Nothing tilts or spins.

Filming notes

  • Shoot every room vertically at 1080 × 1920. Keep the main subject in the middle 690px so the arch doesn’t crop it.
  • Hold the camera still or walk slowly forward. The template adds the push-in.
  • Lauren’s outro is a 3–4 second clip, framed head and shoulders, centered for the circle.
